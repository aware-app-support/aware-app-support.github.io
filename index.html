Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aware work?

Select the apps you want to monitor, choose a daily usage threshold, and write a personal reminder. Aware helps you pause and reflect when your threshold is reached.

Why does Aware request Screen Time access?

Aware uses Apple's Screen Time features to monitor the usage of selected apps and display reminders. This permission is required for its core functionality.

Can I change my settings?

Yes. You can update your selected apps, daily threshold, and personal reminder within Aware.
